🇺🇸 1. What is Independence Day?

Independence Day is an American national holiday celebrated every year on 4 July. It is also called the Fourth of July.

On this day, Americans celebrate the birth of the United States as an independent country. Families and friends meet, have picnics, watch parades and enjoy fireworks. Many houses and streets are decorated with the colours of the American flag.

📜 2. A short history of American independence

In the 18th century, thirteen British colonies existed in North America. Many colonists were unhappy with British taxes and rules. They wanted to make their own decisions.

1775 – The war begins

The American Revolutionary War began between Great Britain and the thirteen colonies.

4 July 1776 – The Declaration of Independence

The Second Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence. It announced that the thirteen colonies were free and independent states.

1781 – The victory at Yorktown

American and French forces won an important victory against the British army at Yorktown.

1783 – The war ends

The Treaty of Paris officially recognised the independence of the United States.

Remember: 4 July 1776 is the date of the Declaration of Independence. The war did not end on that day. It continued until 1783.

Who wrote the Declaration?

📜 Thomas Jefferson

He wrote the first draft of the Declaration of Independence.

🖋️ John Adams

He supported independence and helped promote the Declaration.

🕊️ Benjamin Franklin

He was a diplomat, inventor and one of the important American leaders of the Revolution.

🇵🇱 3. Polish heroes of American independence

Did you know that two famous Polish military leaders helped the Americans during the War of Independence? Their names were Tadeusz Kościuszko and Kazimierz Pułaski.

🏰 Tadeusz Kościuszko

Thaddeus Kosciuszko

He was a Polish military engineer. He helped the American army by designing and building important fortifications. His work at Saratoga and West Point was especially important.

He served in the Continental Army and became a brigadier general. Later, he returned to Poland and led the 1794 uprising against foreign domination.

🐎 Kazimierz Pułaski

Casimir Pulaski

He was a Polish cavalry commander. He joined the American fight for independence in 1777 and helped organise and train American cavalry forces.

He was wounded during the siege of Savannah in 1779 and died shortly afterwards. He is remembered as the Father of the American Cavalry.

Why did they help America?
Both men believed in freedom and fought against oppression. They travelled from Europe to help the American cause. They are remembered as heroes in both Poland and the United States.

🇵🇱 Two countries, one idea: FREEDOM

Kościuszko and Pułaski are important symbols of the historical friendship between Poland and the United States. Their stories remind us that people from different countries can work together for freedom.

🎆 4. How do Americans celebrate?

🎇 Fireworks

Fireworks are one of the most popular parts of Independence Day. Many towns organise big evening firework displays.

🎺 Parades

People watch colourful parades with marching bands, flags, decorated cars and people wearing red, white and blue clothes.

🍔 Picnics and barbecues

Families and friends meet outdoors. They eat hot dogs, hamburgers, corn on the cob, salads and watermelon.

🎵 Music and concerts

There are outdoor concerts and patriotic songs. Some events include the national anthem, “The Star-Spangled Banner”.

⭐ Flags and decorations

People decorate their homes, gardens and streets with American flags, stars and stripes.

📖 6. Reading – My Fourth of July (A2)

Hello! My name is Emma and I live in the United States. My favourite summer holiday is Independence Day. It is on the fourth of July.

In the morning, my family decorates our garden with American flags. My brother wears a blue T-shirt and I wear a red dress. We go to a parade in our town. There are marching bands, colourful balloons and lots of people.

In the afternoon, we have a barbecue with our friends. We eat hamburgers, corn and watermelon. My grandmother tells us stories about American history. She also tells us about two Polish heroes: Thaddeus Kosciuszko and Casimir Pulaski. They helped Americans during the War of Independence.

In the evening, we watch beautiful fireworks. The sky is full of red, white and blue lights. I love this special day!
