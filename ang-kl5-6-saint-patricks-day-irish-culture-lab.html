🇮🇪 1. What is Saint Patrick’s Day?

Saint Patrick’s Day is celebrated every year on 17 March. It is a cultural and religious holiday that honours Saint Patrick, the patron saint of Ireland.

Who was Saint Patrick?

Saint Patrick lived many centuries ago. He was born in Roman Britain, probably in the late fourth century. When he was a teenager, he was taken to Ireland as a captive. After some years, he escaped and returned home.

Later, according to his own writings, he returned to Ireland as a Christian missionary. He helped spread Christianity there. Saint Patrick became one of the most important figures in Irish history and tradition.

Why is 17 March special?

Saint Patrick is traditionally believed to have died on 17 March, around the fifth century. People remember him on this day.

🍀 Did you know?

Saint Patrick’s Day is celebrated in Ireland and in many other countries around the world. Irish communities have helped make this celebration popular internationally.

☘️ 2. Irish Symbols and Traditions

☘️ The shamrock A small three-leaf plant and a symbol of Ireland.
🌈 The rainbow In Irish folklore, a pot of gold may be hidden at its end.
🍀 Four-leaf clover A symbol of good luck.
🧝 The leprechaun A little magical character from Irish folklore.
💚 Green colour A colour strongly connected with Ireland.
🎶 Irish music Traditional songs, dancing and lively celebrations.

How do people celebrate?

  • Wearing green: people wear green clothes, hats and accessories.
  • Parades: many towns and cities organise colourful parades with music and dancing.
  • Irish music: people listen to traditional Irish music and enjoy dancing.
  • Decorations: streets, shops and homes are decorated with shamrocks and green objects.
  • Food: families and friends sometimes enjoy traditional Irish dishes together.

What is a leprechaun?

A leprechaun is a small magical character in Irish folklore. He is often shown wearing green clothes and a hat. According to popular stories, leprechauns hide pots of gold and can be very tricky!

🌈 The legend of the pot of gold

Some stories say that a leprechaun hides a pot of gold at the end of a rainbow. Can you find it? It is a magical legend, not a real treasure map!

🌍 3. Ireland – A Beautiful Country

🏙️ Dublin The capital city of Ireland.
🌊 Cliffs High rocks beside the sea.
🏰 Castles Old buildings with a long history.
🐑 Green fields Beautiful landscapes with grass and hills.

Ireland is an island in north-western Europe. Its capital city is Dublin. Ireland is famous for its green landscapes, old castles, music, literature and friendly cultural traditions.

The Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland are two different political parts of the island. Northern Ireland is part of the United Kingdom.

📖 5. Reading – A Green Day in Dublin

Tom’s Saint Patrick’s Day

Tom lives in Dublin, the capital city of Ireland. Every year, on 17 March, he celebrates Saint Patrick’s Day with his family and friends.

In the morning, Tom puts on a green T-shirt, a funny green hat and a shamrock badge. His little sister wears green socks and a green scarf.

They go to the city centre to watch the Saint Patrick’s Day parade. There are colourful floats, musicians, dancers and lots of happy people. Tom loves the traditional Irish music. He takes many photos with his phone.

After the parade, the family goes home. They eat a delicious meal and listen to Irish songs. Tom's grandmother tells them stories about leprechauns and their pots of gold.

Tom thinks Saint Patrick’s Day is fantastic. He loves the green decorations, the music and the happy atmosphere. He says: “Ireland is a wonderful place!”
