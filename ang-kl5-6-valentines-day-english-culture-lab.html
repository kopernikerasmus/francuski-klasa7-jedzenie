❤️ 1. What is Valentine’s Day?

Valentine’s Day is celebrated on 14 February every year. It is a day associated with love, friendship and kindness.

People often send cards, give flowers and chocolates, or spend time with someone special. In many schools, children exchange Valentine’s cards with their classmates and friends.

Who was Saint Valentine?

Saint Valentine was an early Christian figure associated with the history of this celebration. There are several stories about him, and historians do not know all the details of his life for certain.

One popular legend says that Valentine secretly married couples when the Roman emperor did not allow some soldiers to marry. This story helped connect his name with love and romance.

How did it become a romantic celebration?

During the Middle Ages, people in Europe began to connect 14 February with romantic love. Later, sending written Valentine’s messages became popular. In the nineteenth century, printed Valentine’s cards became much more common.

Did you know?

Valentine’s Day is not only about romantic love. It can also be a celebration of friendship, family and showing people that we care about them.

🌹 2. Valentine’s Day Traditions

💌Valentine’s cardA card with a kind or loving message.
🌹Red rosesFlowers often connected with love.
🍫ChocolatesA popular Valentine’s gift.
🧸Teddy bearA soft toy given as a present.
💝A giftSomething you give to another person.
💐A bouquetA group of beautiful flowers.

Valentine’s Day in English-speaking countries

  • In the United States: children sometimes exchange Valentine’s cards or small treats at school. People may go out for a special dinner.
  • In the United Kingdom: people send cards, flowers and chocolates. Some cards are signed anonymously.
  • In Canada and Australia: people also celebrate with cards, flowers and small gifts. The day is often an opportunity to show appreciation.

Traditions can be different from family to family. Not everyone celebrates Valentine’s Day in the same way.

📖 4. Reading – A Special Day

A Valentine’s Day at School

It is 14 February. Emma is very excited because it is Valentine’s Day. She is at school with her best friend, Lily.

In the morning, their teacher puts a big pink box on the desk. It is the Valentine’s mailbox! Students can put cards inside it. Some cards have names, but others are anonymous.

Emma gives Lily a beautiful card. It has a red heart and the words: “You are a wonderful friend!” Lily smiles and gives Emma a small box of chocolates.

After lunch, the teacher reads some kind messages. Everyone is happy. Emma thinks that Valentine’s Day is not only about presents. It is also about friendship, kindness and making other people smile.
