🏰 1. What is the British monarchy?

The United Kingdom is a constitutional monarchy. The monarch is the head of state, but Parliament and the government have important political powers.

👑 The monarch

The King or Queen is the head of state.

🏛️ Parliament

Parliament debates and makes laws.

📜 The constitution

The monarch has constitutional and ceremonial duties.

Remember! The United Kingdom is not the same as the Commonwealth. The Commonwealth is an association of independent countries.

👑 2. The Royal Family today

Meet some members of the Royal Family. The photographs below are official coronation portraits and historical family photographs.

King Charles III

Charles became King on 8 September 2022 after the death of his mother, Queen Elizabeth II. His coronation took place on 6 May 2023.

King since 2022

Queen Camilla

Camilla is the wife of King Charles III. Her official title is The Queen.

Prince William

William is the Prince of Wales and the heir to the throne. He is the elder son of King Charles III.

Catherine

Catherine is William's wife. She is the Princess of Wales.

Princess Anne

Anne is the King's sister. Her title is the Princess Royal.

Prince Harry

Harry is the younger son of King Charles III. He married Meghan in 2018. They stepped back from senior working royal roles in 2020.

Prince Edward

Edward is the King's younger brother. His title is the Duke of Edinburgh.

Sarah Ferguson

Sarah was married to Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor. They have two daughters, Beatrice and Eugenie.

William and Catherine's children: Prince George (born 2013), Princess Charlotte (born 2015) and Prince Louis (born 2018). Harry and Meghan's children are Archie (born 2019) and Lilibet (born 2021).

🔎 3. Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor

Important update: In November 2025, Andrew lost the title of Prince and the style “His Royal Highness”. His current name is Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor.

👨 Family

Andrew is the second son of Queen Elizabeth II and Prince Philip. He is the younger brother of King Charles III.

👩 Former wife

He married Sarah Ferguson in 1986. They divorced in 1996.

👸 Daughters

His daughters are Princess Beatrice and Princess Eugenie. They retain their princess titles.

Andrew no longer performs official royal duties. His former title Duke of York was removed from the Roll of the Peerage.

Remember: losing a royal title does not automatically remove a person from the line of succession. The official Royal Family website lists Andrew in eighth place. A change to the succession requires legislation.

The current official list and title information should be checked periodically because royal status and succession rules can change.

🏰 5. The royal dynasties – portraits and history

A dynasty is a family that provides a series of rulers. Explore some important monarchs from different royal houses.

House of Normandy

1066–1135

William the Conqueror won the Battle of Hastings in 1066 and became King of England.

Norman Conquest

Angevin / Plantagenet

1154–1399

Henry II began the royal line often called the Plantagenets. His sons included Richard I and John.

Richard I – Lionheart

King 1189–1199

He was famous for his military campaigns and his role in the Third Crusade.

King John

King 1199–1216

In 1215 he agreed to Magna Carta, an important charter in English constitutional history.

Plantagenet kings

13th–14th centuries

Edward III strengthened royal authority and was an important figure in the Hundred Years' War.

House of York

1461–1485, with interruptions

Richard III was the last Yorkist king. He died at the Battle of Bosworth in 1485.

House of Tudor

1485–1603

Henry VII founded the Tudor dynasty after the Wars of the Roses.

Henry VIII

King 1509–1547

He had six wives. During his reign, England broke with the authority of the Pope.

Elizabeth I

Queen 1558–1603

Her reign is often called the Elizabethan Age. English theatre and exploration developed significantly.

House of Stuart

1603–1714, with interruption

James VI of Scotland became James I of England in 1603. The two kingdoms shared one monarch.

House of Hanover

1714–1901

Queen Victoria ruled for more than 63 years. The British Empire expanded greatly during her reign.

Saxe-Coburg and Gotha / Windsor

1901–today

Edward VII belonged to Saxe-Coburg and Gotha. George V renamed the royal house Windsor in 1917.

Queen Elizabeth II

Queen 1952–2022

She was the longest-reigning British monarch. Her reign lasted more than 70 years.

🏰 6. Royal castles and palaces

Windsor Castle

A royal residence with a history of almost 1,000 years.

Buckingham Palace

The King's official London residence and an important place for royal ceremonies.

Tower of London

A historic fortress, palace and prison. The Crown Jewels are kept here.

Westminster Abbey

The traditional church for British coronations and the burial place of many famous people.

📖 9. Reading – The British Royal Family

The United Kingdom is a constitutional monarchy. The monarch is the head of state, but Parliament and the government have important political powers.

King Charles III became King in September 2022. His wife is Queen Camilla. Charles has two sons: William and Harry. William is the Prince of Wales and the heir to the throne. He and Catherine have three children: George, Charlotte and Louis.

Harry is married to Meghan. They have two children, Archie and Lilibet. In 2020, Harry and Meghan stepped back from their roles as senior working royals.

Charles also has a sister, Princess Anne, and two brothers, Andrew and Edward. Andrew is now known as Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor. In November 2025, he lost the title of Prince and the style of Royal Highness. His daughters are Princess Beatrice and Princess Eugenie.

Many royal houses ruled England and Scotland. The Tudors, Stuarts, Hanovers and Windsors are some of the best-known dynasties.
