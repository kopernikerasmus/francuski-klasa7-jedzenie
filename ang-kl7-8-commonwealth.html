🌍 1. What is the Commonwealth?

The Commonwealth of Nations is a voluntary association of 56 independent countries.

Po polsku: Wspólnota Narodów to dobrowolne stowarzyszenie 56 niezależnych państw, które współpracują ze sobą.

🌍 🌎 🌏

56 independent countries

About 2.7 billion people

5 geographical regions

Remember!
  • Commonwealth members are independent countries.
  • They are equal members of the organisation.
  • They work together on common goals.
  • They are not ruled by the British government.
Important: The Commonwealth is NOT the British Empire. It is a voluntary association of independent countries.

📜 2. A little history

The modern Commonwealth began in 1949, with the London Declaration.

🏛️

The British Empire

W przeszłości Wielka Brytania posiadała imperium kolonialne. Wiele dzisiejszych państw Commonwealth było kiedyś częścią tego imperium.

🕊️

Independence

Państwa stopniowo uzyskiwały niepodległość i zaczęły samodzielnie decydować o swojej przyszłości.

🤝

1949 – a new beginning

London Declaration stworzyła podstawy nowoczesnej Wspólnoty. Republiki mogły pozostać jej członkami.

🌍

Today

Wspólnota zrzesza 56 niezależnych państw z różnych kontynentów i kultur.

Interesting fact: India became independent in 1947. It wanted to become a republic and still remain in the Commonwealth. The 1949 London Declaration made this possible.

👑 4. The King and the Commonwealth

King Charles III is the Head of the Commonwealth. To rola symboliczna, która podkreśla jedność organizacji.

👑

Head of the Commonwealth

Król Karol III pełni symboliczną funkcję głowy Wspólnoty.

🏛️

Independent governments

Każde państwo członkowskie ma własny rząd i podejmuje własne decyzje.

🗳️

Republics and monarchies

Wspólnota obejmuje zarówno republiki, jak i monarchie.

Do not confuse these terms!
  • Head of the Commonwealth – symboliczna funkcja króla w całej organizacji.
  • Head of State – głowa państwa. W wielu członkach Commonwealth jest nią prezydent.
  • Commonwealth realm – niezależne państwo, w którym brytyjski monarcha jest również głową państwa.

What is a Commonwealth realm?

Niektóre państwa Commonwealth mają króla Karola III jako głowę państwa. Inne są republikami i mają własnych prezydentów.

Example: India is a republic. The President of India is its head of state. India is still a member of the Commonwealth.

🧠 8. Did you know?

🌍

Not only former British colonies

Mozambique, Rwanda, Gabon and Togo joined the Commonwealth despite having no historical constitutional link with the British Empire.

🇮🇳

India is the largest

India is the most populous member country of the Commonwealth.

🏝️

Many small island states

There are many small island countries in the Caribbean and Pacific.

🗣️

Many languages

English is important in the organisation, but members speak hundreds of languages.

🕊️

Voluntary membership

Countries choose to be members. The Commonwealth is not a colonial government.

📅

Commonwealth Day

Commonwealth Day is celebrated every year in March.

📖 10. Reading – read the text (A2)

A family of independent countries

Hello! My name is Sophie. I am learning about the Commonwealth.

The Commonwealth is a group of 56 independent countries. They are in Africa, Asia, Europe, the Caribbean and the Pacific.

Many members were once connected with the British Empire. However, the modern Commonwealth is not an empire. Its members are independent and equal.

King Charles III is the Head of the Commonwealth. His role is symbolic. He does not govern the member countries.

Some members are republics, like India. Other members are monarchies. They work together on education, sport, democracy, human rights and climate change.

The Commonwealth Games bring athletes together. Commonwealth Day is celebrated in March.

I think the Commonwealth is an interesting example of international cooperation!

🌟 13. Remember!

  • The Commonwealth is a voluntary association of 56 independent countries.
  • The modern Commonwealth began in 1949.
  • King Charles III is its symbolic Head.
  • Members are independent and equal.
  • The Commonwealth includes republics and monarchies.
  • Members cooperate on democracy, education, sport, human rights and development.
  • The Commonwealth is not the British Empire.
